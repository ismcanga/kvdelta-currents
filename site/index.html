KVDelta Currents

Testing KVDelta against public ocean-current archives first, then prospective near-real-time observations.

K + Δ → V

K is the bounded information known at prediction time. Δ is the additional co-determining dimension. V is the later observable state. The relationship is a hypothesis to test, not an assumed forecasting law.
